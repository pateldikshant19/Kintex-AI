## 📌 SLIDE 4: MULTI-DIMENSIONAL RISK ASSESSMENT

**How We Predict Injury Risk**

**5 Key Factors (Weighted):**

1. **Physical Load (30%)**
   - Training workload
   - Intensity levels
   - Duration

2. **Recovery Quality (25%)**
   - Sleep hours
   - Heart rate variability
   - Muscle soreness

3. **Psychological State (20%)**
   - Fatigue levels
   - Stress indicators
   - Motivation

4. **Environmental Factors (15%)**
   - Weather conditions
   - Travel distance
   - Playing surface

5. **Historical Patterns (10%)**
   - Past injuries
   - Recovery timelines
   - Seasonal trends

**Result:** Risk Score (0-100) → 🟢 LOW | 🟡 MEDIUM | 🔴 HIGH

**Key Differentiator:**  
"Competitors use single-factor analysis. We use multi-dimensional assessment for 90%+ accuracy vs. industry standard 70-80%."
